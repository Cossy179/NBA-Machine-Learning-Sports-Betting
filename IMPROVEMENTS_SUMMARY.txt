📈 EXPECTED VALUE & BETTING STRATEGY
================================================================================

EXPECTED VALUE (EV):
   • EV > 0.05: Great bet (take it!)
   • EV > 0: Good bet (profitable long-term)
   • EV = 0: Fair bet (break-even)
   • EV < 0: Bad bet (losing money long-term)

KELLY CRITERION:
   • Optimal bet sizing based on edge
   • Typically 0-5% of bankroll
   • Never bet more than suggested
   • Protects against ruin

RISK MANAGEMENT:
   • Risk Score < 0.5: Moderate (acceptable)
   • Risk Score > 0.7: High (consider skipping)
   • Diversify across multiple parlays
   • Track results to validate model
